\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CSD yields two findings.
First, the \emph{Likert Blindspot}: pairwise and Likert scoring produce divergent verdicts---complete at moderate bias ($d \to 0$ at $n = 355$), ${>}60\%$ signal loss at stronger effects---across four judges (Gemini weaker on formality), three benchmarks, and seven methods.
Second, GEE decomposition reveals distinct per-axis confound structures---verbosity is style-dominated, formality conditional, register judge-dependent.
Non-tie concordance (76.6\%, $p < .001$) is more consistent with attenuation than construct divergence; single-format audits risk systematic blind spots.
